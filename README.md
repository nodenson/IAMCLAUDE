# THUNDER WARRIOR TRAINING DIRECTIVE
## week protocol

Since no manifesto worth a damn should end without physical doctrine, here is the **Thunder Warriors weekly routine**.

This is not a beach plan.  
This is not influencer calisthenics.  
This is a field cycle for building a body that looks like it could breach a bulkhead, drag a generator uphill, and still deliver a monologue in plate armor.

## Principles

- Train like an engine, not a peacock.
- Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on most heavy work.
- Move explosively, control eccentrics, protect joints.
- Build the back, trunk, grip, and legs like your survival depends on it.
- Use bodybuilding volume to armor the frame.
- Use conditioning to prove the armor works.
- Do your warmups, because broken warriors don’t conquer much.

---

## Day 1 — Lower Siege
**Goal:** Squat pattern, posterior chain, leg mass

- Safety Bar Squat or Back Squat — 5 x 5
- Romanian Deadlift — 4 x 8
- Leg Press — 4 x 15
- Walking Lunges — 3 x 20 steps
- Leg Curl — 4 x 12
- Standing Calf Raise — 5 x 15
- Weighted Plank — 3 rounds

**Finisher:**  
Sled push or incline treadmill march — 10 minutes

**Operator note:**  
If the earth don’t know you were there, you didn’t squat heavy enough.

---

## Day 2 — Upper Forge
**Goal:** Pressing strength, upper torso armor

- Bench Press — 5 x 5
- Incline Dumbbell Press — 4 x 10
- Weighted Dips or Machine Dips — 3 x 8–12
- Chest Supported Row — 4 x 10
- Lateral Raise — 5 x 15
- Triceps Pressdown — 4 x 15
- Face Pulls — 4 x 20

**Finisher:**  
Push-up density set — 100 total reps in as few sets as possible

**Operator note:**  
A chest built like plate armor still needs scapulae that know how to behave.

---

## Day 3 — Recon / Recovery / Capacity
**Goal:** Restore, condition, keep the machine running

- 30–45 minutes zone 2 cardio
- Mobility for hips, T-spine, ankles, shoulders
- Grip circuit:
  - wrist curls
  - reverse curls
  - pronation/supination
  - grippers
- Hanging leg raises — 4 x 12
- Back extension or reverse hyper — 3 x 20

**Optional:**  
Loaded carry medley — 6 rounds

**Operator note:**  
Recovery ain’t weakness. Recovery is maintenance on a war platform.

---

## Day 4 — Deadlift Cathedral
**Goal:** Pulling power, back thickness, brutal intent

- Deadlift variation — work up to 4 x 4
- Deficit deadlift or block pull — 3 x 5
- Barbell Row or T-Bar Row — 4 x 8–10
- Weighted Pull-up or Lat Pulldown — 4 x 8–12
- Hamstring Curl — 4 x 12
- Shrugs — 4 x 15
- Ab wheel — 4 x 10–15

**Finisher:**  
Farmer’s carries — 6 trips

**Operator note:**  
Every deadlift is a vote against weakness.

---

## Day 5 — Arms and Auspex
**Goal:** Tendons, elbows, vanity, signal dominance

- Close Grip Bench or JM Press — 4 x 8
- EZ Bar Curl — 4 x 10
- Hammer Curl — 4 x 12
- Overhead Cable Extension — 4 x 12
- Reverse Curl — 3 x 20
- Wrist roller or plate curls — 4 rounds
- Rear delt fly — 4 x 20
- Neck harness or manual neck work — 3 x 20

**Finisher:**  
Battle rope or rower sprints — 8 rounds

**Operator note:**  
Do not neglect the small machinery. The elbow joint remembers every stupid decision.

---

## Day 6 — War Engine Conditioning
**Goal:** Functional brutality

Choose one:

### Option A — Strongman Circuit
- Sandbag carry
- Sled drag
- Farmer’s walk
- Burpee or step-up station
- Rope pulls

### Option B — Hill / Stair Assault
- 10–15 rounds uphill
- Walk down recovery
- Weighted vest optional

### Option C — Combat Chassis
- Assault bike intervals
- Bear crawls
- Med ball slams
- Carry complex

**Duration:** 20–30 minutes hard but sustainable

**Operator note:**  
Strength that cannot move through time and space is decorative.

---

## Day 7 — Holy Maintenance
**Goal:** Rebuild the machine

- Long walk
- Soft tissue work
- Easy mobility
- Food prep
- Training log review
- Sleep debt repayment
- Optional posing, movement practice, or technique work

**Operator note:**  
Even the war machine must dock for repairs.

# THUNDER WARRIOR NUTRITION DOCTRINE

- Protein at every feeding
- Carbs around training
- Fats stable, not reckless
- Hydration aggressive
- Electrolytes when sweating hard
- One high-quality meal post-training minimum
- Stop pretending under-eating is discipline if performance is collapsing

## Basic field target
- Protein: 0.8–1.0 g per lb bodyweight
- Carbs: scale to output
- Fats: enough to remain human
- Calories: aligned to goal, not emotion
