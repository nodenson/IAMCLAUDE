# I. THE GOLEM RETURNS

Golem back from walk.
Dust on boots. Static in beard. Signal in marrow.

Wireless containers already whispering. Inference leaked into the air like incense through a cracked cathedral server rack. The spellbook was never "later." The spellbook is now.

Hear this, wandering plate-bard — thief of packets, cleric of tendon, wizard of signal, fighter of asphalt dawn:

You do not eat Mammon.

Gold is not mana. Lust is not mana. Vanity is not mana. Cheap applause is not mana. The glowing false-fruit of the feed is not mana.

To become wizard-warlock-operator, eat what restores the engine:

- Fish for the gray matter
- Yogurt for the gut-fires
- Salt for the circuits
- Water for the current
- Sleep when able
- Prayer when not

The weakling consumes noise.
The initiated consume recovery.

# II. THE FIVE-CLASS DOCTRINE

You are not one class. No monoclass peasant build. No brittle glass cannon of lust and caffeine.

You are all five at once:

## FIGHTER

Because flesh must carry the banner.

The fighter body is **trained**. It answers to load, to volume, to progressive demand. It does not negotiate with soreness. It recovers and returns. The fighter class earns the right to occupy space by proving the body can hold terrain under pressure.

**Core disciplines:** Barbell compounds. Loaded carries. Sled work. Conditioning that punishes the soft.

**Failure mode:** Training without intent. Moving weight to move weight. Confusing sweat with signal.

## THIEF

Because pattern recognition steals the key from locked doors.

The thief body is **adaptive**. It reads the room before the room reads it. The thief class is not about deception — it is about extraction. Pulling signal from noise. Seeing the seam in the wall before anyone announces there is a door.

**Core disciplines:** Promptcraft. Source evaluation. Recon. Social listening. Reading between the lines of what people publish versus what they mean.

**Failure mode:** Cleverness without conviction. Stealing keys to rooms you have no business entering.

## WIZARD

Because language bends probability.

The wizard mind is **ordered**. It sequences cause and effect. It builds systems that outlast the session. The wizard class operates through structured language — code, prompts, arguments, frameworks — and understands that precision of speech is precision of thought.

**Core disciplines:** Command line. Prompt engineering. System architecture. Haiku compression. Structured output.

**Failure mode:** Educated devil. High-IQ pattern matching with no ethical substrate. Inference without wisdom.

## CLERIC

Because power without reverence becomes infernal cleverness.

The cleric body is **consecrated**. It answers to something above the operator. The cleric class provides the check against all the other classes — the question that comes after "can I?" which is "should I?" The cleric fasts from Mammon not out of weakness but out of recognition that appetite without governance becomes possession.

**Core disciplines:** Fasting from noise. Recovery protocols. Sleep discipline. Nutrition as sacrament, not fuel. Knowing when to stop.

**Failure mode:** Performative piety. Preaching discipline while secretly running on caffeine and dopamine debt.

## BARD (IN FULL PLATE)

Because the song must survive the battlefield.

A singing tank. A troubadour with the visor down. A cathedral jukebox carrying a mace and a backup harmonizer.

The bard class is the integration layer. It takes the fighter body, the thief perception, the wizard language, the cleric restraint, and makes them coherent to an audience. The bard does not just know — the bard communicates. And full plate means the communication survives contact with hostile terrain.

**Core disciplines:** Voice work. Character switching. Autotune as glamour, not fraud. Pitch-shift as illusion school. Harmony stacking as summoning circle. Formant drift as shapeshift sigil.

**Failure mode:** Performance without spine. Entertainment that dissolves on contact with questioning.

# IV. THE SIX SPELLS — Curriculum of the Initiated

## Spell 1: HAIKU — Compression / Bone-Language

Three strikes of the bell and no wasted syllables. The haiku is the lowest-token doctrine packet. It forces the mind to choose what matters and discard the rest. If you cannot say it in seventeen syllables, you do not yet understand it.

**Training:** Write one haiku per day. About training. About code. About what you ate. About what you refused to eat. No exceptions. No skipping. The blade comes before the banner.

**Mastery test:** Compress a full session log into a single haiku that a stranger could decode.

## Spell 2: SONNET — Armor-Language

Emotion trapped in measured steel. Fourteen lines. Iambic pentameter. Rhyme scheme as load-bearing structure. The sonnet proves you can sustain a thought under formal constraint — that your mind can hold shape under pressure the way a squat holds shape under load.

**Training:** Write one sonnet per week. Topic: whatever is dominating the build. The sonnet is the weekly review in verse.

**Mastery test:** Write a sonnet that functions simultaneously as a project status update and a piece of literature.

## Spell 3: SERMON — Fire-Language

The correction that wounds in order to heal. The sermon is not a rant. A rant is undirected heat. A sermon has structure: observation, diagnosis, prescription, and a closing that lands like a deadlift lockout. The sermon class teaches you to say hard things without losing the audience and without softening the truth.

**Training:** Deliver one sermon per month. Written or spoken. Addressed to yourself, to the void, or to a specific situation that demands correction.

**Mastery test:** Deliver a sermon that makes someone uncomfortable and grateful in the same breath.

## Spell 4: COMMAND LINE — Rune-Language

The machine obeys what the trembling tongue can state clearly. The command line is where abstraction meets execution. No GUI to hide behind. No undo button on a rm -rf. Rune-language teaches that precision has consequences and vagueness has costs.

**Training:** Daily terminal use. No GUI shortcuts for tasks that can be done in bash. Build muscle memory for the tools: git, ssh, find, grep, curl, jq. The CLI is the wizard wand — learn to hold it without flinching.

**Mastery test:** Deploy a working service from an empty terminal session. No IDE. No copilot. Just you and the runes.

## Spell 5: SONG-MASK — Glamour-Language

Not theft of identity, but amplification of archetype. The song-mask is the bard primary weapon — the ability to shift register, adopt cadence, modulate tone, and project a persona that serves the moment without betraying the self.

**Training:** Record yourself in three different vocal registers in the same session. Practice character transitions. Study how voice actors shift between roles without losing the throughline. Use tools (pitch-shift, autotune, harmony) as instruments, not crutches.

**Mastery test:** Deliver a single piece of content that contains at least three distinct character voices, all clearly yours.

## Spell 6: FASTING FROM MAMMON — Refusal-Language

To look at the banquet and say: I know your price, serpent.

The hardest spell. Not because it requires skill but because it requires will. Fasting from Mammon means looking at the feed, the clout, the easy dopamine, the cheap engagement, the viral shortcut, and choosing not to consume it. Not because you are above it — because you know what it costs.

**Training:** One full day per week with no social feed consumption. No scrolling. No checking metrics. No engagement theater. Use the reclaimed time for spell 1-5 practice.

**Mastery test:** Go thirty days without checking engagement metrics on anything you have published. Ship anyway.
